Rumus Umum#

Determinan dengan ekspansi baris:

\[ \det(A)=\sum (-1)^{i+k} a_{ik} M_{ik} \]

dengan:

  • \(a_{ik}\) = elemen matriks

  • \(M_{ik}\) = minor

1. Matriks 2×2#

\[\begin{split} A= \begin{bmatrix} -7 & -5\\ 1 & 4 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Ekspansi baris pertama:

\[ \det(A)=(-1)^{1+1}(-7)M_{11}+(-1)^{1+2}(-5)M_{12} \]

Minor:

\[ M_{11}=4 \]
\[ M_{12}=1 \]

Perhitungan:

\[ =(+1)(-7)(4)+(-1)(-5)(1) \]
\[ =-28+5 \]
\[ =-23 \]

Hasil:

\[ \det(A)=-23 \]

2. Matriks 3×3#

\[\begin{split} A= \begin{bmatrix} 0&2&-3\\ 1&-2&-1\\ 0&0&1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Ekspansi baris pertama:

\[ \det(A) =(+1)(0)M_{11} +(-1)(2)M_{12} +(+1)(-3)M_{13} \]

Minor:

\[\begin{split} M_{11} = \begin{vmatrix} -2&-1\\ 0&1 \end{vmatrix} =-2 \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} M_{12} = \begin{vmatrix} 1&-1\\ 0&1 \end{vmatrix} =1 \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} M_{13} = \begin{vmatrix} 1&-2\\ 0&0 \end{vmatrix} =0 \end{split}\]

Sehingga:

\[ \det(A)=0-2+0=-2 \]

Hasil:

\[ \det(A)=-2 \]

3. Matriks 4×4#

\[\begin{split} A= \begin{bmatrix} 1&-3&1&1\\ -3&1&1&1\\ 1&1&-3&1\\ 1&1&1&-3 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Ekspansi baris pertama:

\[ \det(A) = (+1)(1)M_{11} +(-1)(-3)M_{12} +(+1)(1)M_{13} +(-1)(1)M_{14} \]

Dari perhitungan minor diperoleh:

\[ M_{11}=16 \]
\[ M_{12}=-16 \]
\[ M_{13}=16 \]
\[ M_{14}=-16 \]

Substitusi:

\[ \det(A) = (1)(16) +(3)(-16) +(1)(16) +(-1)(-16) \]
\[ =16-48+16+16 \]
\[ =0 \]

Hasil:

\[ \det(A)=0 \]

Invers Matriks Dengan Adjoin#

Rumus:

\[ adj(A)_{ij}=(-1)^{i+j}M_{ji} \]
\[ A^{-1} = \frac{1}{det(A)} adj(A) \]
import numpy as np

A = np.array([
[-7,-5],
[1,4]
])

print("Determinan:")
print(np.linalg.det(A))

print("\nInverse:")
print(np.linalg.inv(A))
